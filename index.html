Install DeskDrop App

Install DeskDrop as a standalone app on your Mac, Windows, or phone. It opens in its own window, launches instantly from your desktop or home screen, and works offline.

In Safari: tap Share ⎋ and select "Add to Home Screen" or "Add to Dock".
In Chrome/Edge: click the Install icon in the address bar.
